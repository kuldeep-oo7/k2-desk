01 / BOTH DIRECTIONS

Trend pullback

Established uptrend or downtrend

Trigger
A pullback toward the 10-bar average is followed by a completed candle beyond the prior candle's high/low.
Invalidation
Beyond the last three candles' pullback extreme.
Exit
Just before the prior 20-bar swing high/low.
What can fail
The pullback develops into a reversal, or there is too little room before the prior swing.

02 / BOTH DIRECTIONS

Structure breakout

A clearly defined prior boundary

Trigger
A completed candle closes beyond the previous 20-bar boundary with at least 1.5 times the median of six prior activity readings.
Invalidation
Beyond the breakout candle's opposite extreme.
Exit
Project the height of the previous range beyond its broken boundary.
What can fail
A false break, thin activity or a late entry consumes the available reward.

03 / BOTH DIRECTIONS

Failed breakout

An attempted escape returns inside a prior range

Trigger
The previous candle crossed a prior boundary and closed inside again; the latest candle confirms the rejection.
Invalidation
Beyond the failed excursion.
Exit
Just before the opposite boundary of the previous range.
What can fail
The apparent rejection is only a pause before the original breakout resumes.

04 / BOTH DIRECTIONS

Range bounce

Repeated boundaries, low directional efficiency

Trigger
A directional candle reacts near an edge, with room to the opposite edge; entries in the middle are excluded.
Invalidation
Beyond the rejected range edge or signal candle, whichever is farther.
Exit
Just before the opposite range boundary.
What can fail
A range becomes a trend and the boundary gives way.
