HOW THIS IS SCORED. Every price this page freezes is bought on paper at the moment it is shown. Each build then checks the market, using the day’s high and low so a move between two builds is not missed. Every ambiguity counts against the record: a target and a stop in the same window is a stop; a target in the same window as the fill is not counted; a gap through the stop exits at the open; a fill is always at the limit, never better; a price from before the level was shown can never fill it. Unfilled day orders die at 15:30. Futures are held at most 20 sessions; options until expiry.
