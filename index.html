HOW THIS IS SCORED. Every price this page freezes is bought on paper at the moment it is shown. Each build then checks the market, using the day’s high and low so a move between two builds is not missed. Every ambiguity counts against the record: a target and a stop in the same window is a stop; a target in the same window as the fill is not counted; a gap through the stop exits at the open; a fill is always at the limit, never better; a price from before the level was shown can never fill it. Unfilled day orders die at 15:30. Futures are held at most 20 sessions; options until expiry. Stored in the private repository under docs/ledger/, committed on every build, and copied to the Google Sheet.

Premiums and the futures prices they are measured against both come from that same settlement file, so they describe the same moment. Check the price on your broker before placing anything. Net figures are after brokerage, STT at 0.15%, exchange, stamp and GST; the spread is extra and is only known where a live book was seen.
NO SHOCK SHIELD. Nothing on this page stops buys on a bad global morning — not a GIFT Nifty gap, a crude or S&P move, or an India VIX spike. Those signals were tested on NSE’s own data and failed (or had too few events to test), so they do not gate anything. Check the market yourself before acting on a shock day.
WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH. That any of it makes money. The paper ledger so far: futures 38 targets and 221 stops, 14% hit (95% range 6.5–21.4%); short futures 53 targets and 47 stops, 52% hit (95% range 27.6–76.3%); calls 316 targets and 1109 stops, 21.3% hit (95% range 14.6–28%); puts 1427 targets and 1013 stops, 57.2% hit (95% range 44.6–69.9%), over only 18 sessions of the same market. That says how this market went, not that a rule works — each row shows its own break-even. And every test fixed in advance so far, on data never used to build the rule, found no edge: the desk's own rating (replayed on six years of NSE data: −0.03% a trade on its buys, and its SELL/REDUCE shorts LOST −0.14% a trade, t = −3.3), 12-1 momentum, time-series momentum, low volatility, a 10-month market trend filter, and the NIFTY trend reader that now chooses between buys and puts (selling NIFTY in its downtrends: final year t = 0.21) — all six FAIL. The ranking is built on things that were measured, which is not the same as an edge that was proven. Size accordingly.
